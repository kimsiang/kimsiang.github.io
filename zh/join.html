03How to apply如何申请

Three steps三步走

  1. 01

    Read about our four research pillars and pick one or two that excite you.阅读课题组的四大研究方向，选出一两个你感兴趣的。

  2. 02

    Email Kim with a short CV, which programme or position you have in mind, and a few lines on why.给Kim发一封邮件：附简短简历，说明你想申请的项目或岗位，以及简要理由。

  3. 03

    We reply with a time to talk — by video or at the lab — and, for students, a small starter task.我们会约一个时间交流（线上或来实验室），学生还会收到一个小的入门任务。

Email Kim给Kim发邮件 →Read the research first先看研究方向

We supervise in English, Chinese, Malay and Japanese (and a little German). Group culture: weekly muon-physics journal club, a textbook reading group, at least two conference talks a year, and an open, supportive lab.我们可以用英语、中文、马来语和日语（以及少量德语）进行指导。课题组文化：每周缪子物理Journal Club、教材研读小组、每年至少两次会议报告，以及开放、支持性的实验室氛围。
