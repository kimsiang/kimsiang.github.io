27th J-PARC Muon g-2/EDM collaboration meeting at Niigata University第27届 J-PARC 缪子 g-2/EDM 合作组会议在新潟大学举行

Meng attended the 27th J-PARC Muon g-2/EDM Collaboration Meeting at Niigata University and gave a talk on the status of the muon polarization monitor. Exchanges between the Shanghai Muon Physics Group and the KEK/J-PARC muon physics group have become a regular occurrence, and we look forward to an even stronger collaboration in the future!

吕濛参加了在新潟大学举行的第27届 J-PARC 缪子 g-2/EDM 合作组会议，并作了关于缪子极化监测器进展的报告。上海缪子物理课题组与 KEK/J-PARC 缪子物理团队之间的交流已日益常态化，期待未来双方开展更加紧密的合作！
