NuFact 2024: The 25th International Workshop on Neutrinos from Accelerators第25届加速器中微子国际研讨会（NuFact 2024）

Kim Siang participated in the NuFact 2024 conference (September 16–21) at Argonne National Laboratory, both as a convener of Working Group 4 (Muon Physics) and as a speaker on the current status of the PSI muEDM experiment. It was great to be back in the US for the first time in five years and to reconnect with many long-time friends from Japan, Europe, and the US. Besides the rich physics program, the conference offered tours of the DUNE cathode production factory, the Argonne Wakefield Accelerator (AWA), the Advanced Photon Source (APS), and the Center for Nanoscale Materials (CNM). More information about the conference can be found here: https://indico.fnal.gov/event/63406/.

9月16日至21日，许金祥参加了在美国阿贡国家实验室举行的 NuFact 2024 会议，担任第四工作组（缪子物理）召集人，并作了关于 PSI muEDM 实验现状的报告。时隔五年再次赴美，并与来自日本、欧洲和美国的许多老朋友重聚，令人十分欣喜。除了丰富的物理议程外，会议还安排参观了 DUNE 阴极生产工厂、阿贡尾场加速器（AWA）、先进光子源（APS）以及纳米材料中心（CNM）。更多会议信息请见：https://indico.fnal.gov/event/63406/。
