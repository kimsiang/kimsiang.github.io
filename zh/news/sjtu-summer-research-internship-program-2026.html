SJTU Summer Research Internship Program 20262026年上海交通大学暑期科研实习项目

We are accepting students for the SJTU Summer Research Internship this summer! Our project is Fundamental and Applied Muon Physics, which covers the measurement of muon properties with tabletop experiments and 3D density imaging with muography detectors.

Eligibility requirements:
– Applicants must have completed at least one year of an undergraduate program and be currently enrolled as undergraduates;
– Applicants must hold a GPA of at least 3.0 on a 4.0 scale, or equivalent;
– Applicants from non-English-speaking countries must provide an English language proficiency certificate.

The program runs from July 13 to August 23, 2026. Please apply by May 30, 2026 for your application to be considered.

More information about the internship (Project 161) can be found on the program website: https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/summer-program/index.php.

项目时间为2026年7月13日至8月23日。请于2026年5月30日前提交申请。

有关本实习项目（项目编号161）的更多信息，请访问项目网站：https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/summer-program/index.php。
