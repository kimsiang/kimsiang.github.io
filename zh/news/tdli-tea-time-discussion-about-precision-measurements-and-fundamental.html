TDLI Tea Time Discussion about “Precision Measurements and Fundamental Physics”李政道研究所 Tea Time：“精密测量与基础物理”主题讨论

Kim-Siang led a discussion on “Precision Measurements and Fundamental Physics” at the Friday TDLI Tea Time of the new 2021 semester. Measurements such as the electron g-factor, muon g-2, electron mass, and fine-structure constant were discussed and generated a lot of interest from the audience.

在2021年新学期的李政道研究所周五茶歇（Tea Time）活动中，许金祥主持了以“精密测量与基础物理”为主题的讨论。讨论涉及电子 g 因子、缪子 g-2、电子质量和精细结构常数等测量，引起了听众的浓厚兴趣。
