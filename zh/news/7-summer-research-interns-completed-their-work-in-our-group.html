7 Summer Research Interns Completed Their Work in Our Group七名暑期科研实习生在课题组圆满完成实习

This summer, the Muon Physics Group at the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute (TDLI) had the pleasure of hosting seven talented students from around the world for internship projects under the supervision of Prof. Kim Siang Khaw.

Our interns came from diverse backgrounds: Phuriphat Intanit (Nice) from Suranaree University of Technology, Thailand; Martina Pratesi and Yuting Gademski from University College London (UCL), UK; Pedro Salvatico from Faculdade Israelita de Ciências da Saúde Albert Einstein, Brazil; Jing Tian Yong and Junyi Gu from Xiamen University Malaysia, Malaysia; and Qi Jie Tin from Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, Malaysia.

Throughout the summer, they actively contributed to a range of projects spanning muon physics, detector development, and data analysis: Phuriphat (Nice) completed a study on g-factor measurement using an in-house-built solenoid and conducted a radiation study for the SHINE facility; Martina worked on the muography project, characterizing detector performance based on scintillating materials coupled with SiPMs and a CAEN-based data acquisition system; Pedro explored the use of machine learning techniques to enhance density inference in muon tomography, a promising approach using naturally occurring cosmic-ray muons; and Jing Tian, Junyi, and Qi Jie focused on particle detection studies, characterizing SiPM operation and gaining hands-on experience with electronics for particle detection.

The interns joined at different times throughout the summer, creating a lively, collaborative environment full of learning and discovery. They leave the group with valuable research experience, new technical skills, and great memories.

We thank all seven interns for their dedication and enthusiasm, and we wish them every success in their future academic journeys!

今年夏天，李政道研究所缪子物理课题组迎来了七位来自世界各地的优秀学生，在许金祥教授的指导下开展实习项目。

实习生们背景多元：Phuriphat Intanit（Nice）来自泰国苏兰拉里理工大学；Martina Pratesi和Yuting Gademski来自英国伦敦大学学院（UCL）；Pedro Salvatico来自巴西阿尔伯特·爱因斯坦以色列健康科学学院；杨景天和谷骏逸来自马来西亚厦门大学马来西亚分校；Qi Jie Tin来自马来西亚马来西亚理工大学。

整个暑期，他们积极参与了涵盖缪子物理、探测器研发和数据分析的多个项目：Phuriphat（Nice）利用自制螺线管完成了g因子测量研究，并开展了SHINE 装置辐射研究；Martina参与缪子成像项目，对基于闪烁材料耦合硅光电倍增管（SiPM）并采用CAEN 数据采集系统的探测器进行了性能表征；Pedro探索利用机器学习技术提升缪子断层成像中的密度反演能力——这是一种利用天然宇宙线缪子的前景广阔的方法；杨景天、谷骏逸和Qi Jie则专注于粒子探测研究，对 SiPM 的工作特性进行了表征，并获得了粒子探测电子学方面的实践经验。

实习生们在暑期不同时间段陆续加入，营造了充满学习与探索氛围的活跃协作环境。他们在课题组收获了宝贵的科研经历、新的技术技能和美好的回忆。

感谢七位实习生的投入与热情，祝愿他们在今后的学术道路上一切顺利！
