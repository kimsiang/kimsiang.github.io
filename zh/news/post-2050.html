Particle Physics and Nuclear Physics Shanghai Symposium 20242024年粒子物理与核物理上海研讨会

Dr. Siew Yan Hoh recently participated in the Particle Physics and Nuclear Physics Shanghai Symposium 2024, held at Fudan University from December 20 to 22. Representing the Muon Physics Group at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), Dr. Hoh gave a presentation titled “Progress in SJTU Muon Physics Research”, highlighting the group’s contributions to muon physics.

The presentation covered the group’s involvement in major international collaborations, including the Muon g-2 experiment at Fermilab, where our group contributes to the precision measurement of the muon’s magnetic dipole moment through the anomalous precession analysis and beam dynamics corrections. New results, expected in 2025, are anticipated to improve the precision by a factor of two. Dr. Hoh also discussed the group’s contributions to the muon electric dipole moment (muEDM) experiment at PSI, where recent test beam campaigns demonstrated a prototype muon trigger detector with a signal efficiency exceeding 95%.

On the domestic front, the group has been prototyping a high-repetition-rate muon source using the SHINE facility’s electron beam. Preliminary results indicate a yield of approximately 10 million muons per second, which would support applications in μSR studies and muon tomography. The group is also exploring cosmic-ray muography for practical problems such as estimating tunnel overburden.

The symposium provided an excellent platform for collaboration and knowledge exchange, and a great opportunity to present our research progress and meet fellow physicists in the region.

何守仁博士近日参加了2024年12月20日至22日在复旦大学举行的2024年粒子物理与核物理上海研讨会。Hoh博士代表上海交通大学缪子物理课题组作了题为“上海交大缪子物理研究进展”的报告，介绍了课题组在缪子物理领域的工作。

报告介绍了课题组参与的重大国际合作，包括美国费米国家加速器实验室（Fermilab）的缪子反常磁矩（Muon g-2）实验——课题组通过反常进动分析和束流动力学修正，为缪子磁偶极矩的精密测量作出贡献；预计于2025年发布的新结果将把测量精度提高一倍。Hoh博士还介绍了课题组在瑞士保罗谢勒研究所（PSI）缪子电偶极矩（muEDM）实验中的贡献，近期的束流测试表明，原型缪子触发探测器的信号效率超过95%。

在国内，课题组正利用上海高重频硬X射线自由电子激光装置（SHINE）的电子束研制高重频缪子源原型。初步结果表明，缪子产额约为每秒1000万个，可支撑μSR研究和缪子成像等应用。课题组还在探索将宇宙线缪子成像用于隧道上覆层厚度估算等实际问题。

本次研讨会为合作与交流提供了良好平台，也让我们有机会展示研究进展，并与本地区的物理学同行交流。
