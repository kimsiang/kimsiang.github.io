Muography for cities, industry and geoscience面向城市、产业与地球科学的缪子成像

Cosmic-ray muons arrive for free, pass through hundreds of metres of rock, and let us image the inside of large things without touching them. Here is how a project with our group works.宇宙线缪子免费到达、可穿透数百米岩石，让我们无需接触即可对庞大目标内部成像。以下是与课题组合作的方式。

01Service服务

Muography surveys缪子成像勘测

Non-invasive imaging of what lies above or inside large structures: tunnels, river beds, foundations, karst and mine workings. Our pilot in the Shanghai Outer Ring Tunnel tracked the Huangpu tide through the muon rate; in 2026 we surveyed limestone and karst sites across Peninsular Malaysia.对大型结构上方或内部进行无损成像：隧道、河床、地基、岩溶与矿山巷道。在上海外环隧道的试验中，我们通过缪子计数率追踪到黄浦江潮汐；2026年又在马来西亚半岛的石灰岩与岩溶场地开展了勘测。

02Service服务

Compact detector R&D便携探测器研发

Portable scintillator–SiPM muon telescopes, readout electronics and DAQ, built in our own lab from design to deployment, with Geant4 simulation (musrSim-upgrade, CRY/EcoMug) to size a system before anything is built.便携式闪烁体–SiPM缪子望远镜、读出电子学与数据获取系统，在自有实验室从设计到部署一体完成；利用Geant4模拟（musrSim-upgrade、CRY/EcoMug）在动工前完成系统选型。

Joint projects and training联合项目与人才培养

HOWHow a project runs项目流程

  1. 01

    Scoping call需求沟通

    What you need to see, where, and at what resolution.你想看什么、在哪里、需要多高的分辨率。

  2. 02

    Feasibility study可行性模拟

    A Geant4 simulation of your geometry gives expected contrast and exposure time before any hardware moves.对你的几何结构做Geant4模拟，在任何硬件进场前给出预期对比度与曝光时间。

  3. 03

    Pilot measurement现场试验

    A portable telescope on site for weeks to months; data are analysed as they arrive.便携望远镜现场运行数周至数月，数据边采集边分析。

  4. 04

    Report and next steps报告与后续

    Density maps, uncertainties and a recommendation on a longer campaign or a permanent monitor.密度图、不确定度，以及是否开展长期监测的建议。
