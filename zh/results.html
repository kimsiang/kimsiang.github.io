01 · Muon g-2 · Fermilab缪子g-2 · 费米实验室

127 ppb

The muon’s magnetic anomaly, measured to 127 parts per billion缪子反常磁矩测量精度达到十亿分之127

The Fermilab Muon g-2 Experiment’s final result, aμ = 116 592 070.5(14.8) × 10⁻¹¹, is four times more precise than the previous world average. It now agrees with the 2025 Standard Model prediction, which moved after a new evaluation of the hadronic contribution.费米实验室缪子g-2实验最终结果 aμ = 116 592 070.5(14.8) × 10⁻¹¹，将世界平均值精度提高了四倍以上；与采用新强子贡献评估的2025年标准模型预言相符。

OUR ROLE我们的贡献 As members of the Muon g-2 Collaboration, we work mainly on the measurement of the anomalous precession frequency ωa and its beam-dynamics corrections.作为缪子g-2国际合作组成员，我们主要参与反常进动频率ωa的测量及其束流动力学修正。

02 · Muon EDM · Fermilab & PSI缪子电偶极矩 · 费米实验室与PSI

1.1×10⁻¹⁹ e·cm

A tighter direct limit on the muon EDM — and a path to 10⁻²³缪子电偶极矩直接上限进一步收紧——并迈向10⁻²³

Using tracker data from the Fermilab g-2 ring (2019–2020), the collaboration set |dμ| < 1.10 × 10⁻¹⁹ e·cm at 95% CL, improving on Brookhaven’s 2009 limit. The frozen-spin muEDM experiment at PSI aims for 4 × 10⁻²¹ e·cm in Phase I and 6 × 10⁻²³ e·cm in Phase II.利用费米实验室g-2储存环2019–2020年径迹数据，得到 |dμ| < 1.10 × 10⁻¹⁹ e·cm（95%置信度），优于布鲁克海文2009年上限。PSI冻结自旋muEDM实验第一阶段目标灵敏度 4 × 10⁻²¹ e·cm，第二阶段 6 × 10⁻²³ e·cm。

OUR ROLE我们的贡献 We design, build and test the muon entrance trigger detector and its fast front-end electronics for muEDM.我们负责muEDM实验缪子入射触发探测器及其快速前端电子学的设计、建造与测试。

03 · SHINE muon source · ShanghaiSHINE缪子源 · 上海

3×10⁶ μ⁺/s

A 50 kHz pulsed muon beam from an X-ray laser’s electron linac利用X射线自由电子激光电子直线加速器产生50 kHz脉冲缪子束

Simulations show that SHINE’s 8 GeV, 100 pC electron bunches on a copper target can deliver about 60 surface muons per bunch to the end of a beamline — 3 × 10⁶ μ⁺/s at 50 kHz. That rate is comparable to existing muon facilities, with a pulse rate a thousand times higher than proton-driven pulsed sources.模拟表明，SHINE 8 GeV、100 pC电子束团轰击铜靶，每束团约60个表面缪子可传输至束线末端，在50 kHz下对应 3 × 10⁶ μ⁺/s——与现有缪子装置相当，而脉冲频率比质子驱动脉冲源高约千倍。

OUR ROLE我们的贡献 We lead the target, beamline and source design studies.我们主导靶、束线与缪子源的设计研究。

04 · Muography · Shanghai缪子成像 · 上海

Tides

Watching the Huangpu River’s tide from inside a tunnel在隧道中用缪子“看见”黄浦江潮汐

A portable scintillator detector inside the Shanghai Outer Ring Tunnel, an immersed tube beneath the Huangpu River, recorded a muon rate that rises and falls opposite to the water level at a nearby tide gauge. The scan along the tunnel matched sediment data and Geant4 simulations.在黄浦江下的上海外环沉管隧道内，便携式闪烁体探测器测得的缪子计数率与附近潮位站水位呈明显反相关；沿隧道的扫描结果与泥沙数据及Geant4模拟一致。

OUR ROLE我们的贡献 We designed the detector, ran the field campaign and led the analysis.我们设计了探测器、完成现场测量并主导数据分析。

J. Appl. Phys. 2025 · News新闻
