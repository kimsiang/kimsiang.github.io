Open Lab Day at TDLI 20252025年李政道研究所实验室开放日

We’re proud to have joined the 2025 Open Lab Day—“Touching the Invisible Dimensions of the Universe”—hosted by our institute in Zhangjiang. Our team (Jiangtao, Jun Kai, Xingyun, Yinghe, Yonghao) brought the mysteries of particle physics to life with two interactive exhibits, a Cloud Chamber and a Spark Chamber, which captivated over 200 visitors!

The cloud chamber revealed the ethereal trails of cosmic rays and alpha particles, turning invisible subatomic activity into a visual spectacle. Nearby, the spark chamber produced crackling bursts of light triggered by passing particles, sparking awe and curiosity in visitors of all ages. These installations bridged the event’s themes of “极大” (cosmic vastness) and “极小” (subatomic realms), echoing the cutting-edge research, such as dark matter detection and neutrino studies, showcased in the lab tours.

From kids tracing particle paths to adults pondering cosmic riddles, the joy of discovery was universal. Special thanks to our team for turning complex science into wonder! For photos, highlights, and CCTV-2 coverage, see the full recap here: https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/2rI6xrp7U_onqsdfTiRx2w. Together, we’re igniting curiosity—one particle at a time! ✨

我们很荣幸参与了李政道研究所在张江举办的2025年实验室开放日——“触摸宇宙的隐秘维度”。团队成员（王江涛、黄俊凯、黄幸运、王赢鹤、曾永浩）带来了云室和火花室两件互动展品，将粒子物理的奥秘生动呈现，吸引了200多名观众！

云室中，宇宙线和α粒子留下若隐若现的径迹，把不可见的亚原子世界变成了一场视觉盛宴；一旁的火花室则随着粒子穿过迸发出噼啪作响的闪光，令各年龄段的观众惊叹不已、好奇心倍增。这些展品呼应了本次活动“极大”（宇宙之浩瀚）与“极小”（亚原子世界）的主题，也与实验室参观中展示的暗物质探测、中微子研究等前沿工作相得益彰。

从追踪粒子径迹的孩子，到思考宇宙谜题的成年人，探索发现的乐趣人人共享。特别感谢团队成员把复杂的科学变成了惊奇！更多照片、精彩瞬间及央视财经频道（CCTV-2）报道，请见活动回顾：https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/2rI6xrp7U_onqsdfTiRx2w。让我们一起点燃好奇心——从一个粒子开始！✨
