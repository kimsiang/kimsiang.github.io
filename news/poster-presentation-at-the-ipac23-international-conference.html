Poster presentation at the IPAC’23 international conference在IPAC’23国际会议上展示墙报

Meng and Jiangtao presented a poster on our Shanghai Muon Source project at the 14th International Particle Accelerator Conference (IPAC’23), held on May 7–12, 2023, in Venice, Italy. The conference, held on an island in Venice, was attended by over 1,000 participants and featured many talks, poster presentations, and social activities. More information about the conference can be found here: https://www.ipac23.org/.

吕濛和王江涛在2023年5月7日至12日于意大利威尼斯举行的第14届国际粒子加速器大会（IPAC’23）上展示了上海缪子源项目墙报。大会在威尼斯的一座小岛上举行，吸引了1000多名代表参会，安排了丰富的报告、墙报展示和社交活动。大会详情请见：https://www.ipac23.org/。
