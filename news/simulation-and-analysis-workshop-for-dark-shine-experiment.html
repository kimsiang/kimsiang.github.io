Simulation and Analysis Workshop for the Dark SHINE ExperimentDark SHINE 实验模拟与分析研讨会

On August 21, 2020, we successfully held a workshop on simulation and analysis for the Dark SHINE experiment. Presentations covered several aspects of the experiment, followed by a fruitful discussion of its future plans.

2020年8月21日，我们成功举办了 Dark SHINE 实验模拟与分析研讨会。报告涵盖了实验的多个方面，随后与会者围绕实验的未来规划进行了富有成效的讨论。
