第十三届全国粒子物理学术会议

Dr. Cheng Chen and Tianqi Hu presented posters on radiative muon decay and the muon EDM, respectively, at the 13th National Particle Physics Conference (August 16–19, 2021), originally planned for Qingdao. Due to the COVID situation, the conference moved online and the posters were presented virtually.
