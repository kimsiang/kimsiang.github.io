SJTU Summer Research Internship Program 2024

We are accepting students for the SJTU Summer Research Internship this summer! Our project is the measurement of muon properties using tabletop setups in our laboratory at the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute.

Eligibility requirements:
– Applicants must have completed at least one year of an undergraduate program and be currently enrolled as undergraduates;
– Applicants must hold a GPA of at least 3.0 on a 4.0 scale, or equivalent;
– Applicants from non-English-speaking countries must provide an English language proficiency certificate.

The program runs from July 1 to August 11, 2024. Applications must be submitted by May 30 to be considered.

More information about the internship can be found in this brochure: https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/en/news/view/718.
