2025.04.13News新闻

Workshop on a Particle and Nuclear Physics Platform Based on SHINE’s High-Energy, High-Current Polarized Electron Beam基于SHINE高能强流极化电子束的粒子物理核物理平台建设研讨会

Kim Siang recently participated in the Workshop on the Development of a Particle and Nuclear Physics Platform Based on SHINE’s High-Energy, High-Current Polarized Electron Beam, held at Fudan University on April 12–13, 2025. He gave a talk on the muon tomography system for the study of the calorimeter muon rejection rate and also discussed the SHINE Muon Source. More information about the workshop can be found at https://imp.fudan.edu.cn/10/de/c45420a725214/page.htm.

2025年4月12日至13日，基于SHINE高能强流极化电子束的粒子物理核物理平台建设研讨会在复旦大学举行，许金祥应邀参会。他作了关于用于研究量能器缪子排除率的缪子成像系统的报告，并介绍了SHINE缪子源。会议详情请见：https://imp.fudan.edu.cn/10/de/c45420a725214/page.htm。
