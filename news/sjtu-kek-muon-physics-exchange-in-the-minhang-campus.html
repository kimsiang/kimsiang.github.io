SJTU-KEK Muon Physics Exchange on the Minhang Campus上海交通大学–KEK缪子物理交流活动在闵行校区举行

Prof. Tsutomu Mibe (KEK and Univ. of Tokyo) and Dr. Yusuke Takeuchi (Kyushu University) visited Shanghai on April 13–14, 2023, for the 2nd meeting (and the 1st in person) of the SJTU-KEK Muon Physics Exchange Program, supported by the NSFC and the JSPS Academic Exchange Program. We shared our recent progress in muon physics experiments and had lunch together at GIMLID Pizza on the SJTU Minhang Campus. Prof. Liang Li, Prof. Kim Siang Khaw, Guan Ming Wong, Meng Lv, Jiangtao Wang, Yonghao Zeng, and Zejia Lu joined the academic exchange lunch session.

2023年4月13日至14日，三部勉教授（KEK及东京大学）和竹内佑甫博士（九州大学）访问上海，参加上海交通大学–KEK缪子物理交流项目第二次会议（也是首次线下会议）。该项目得到国家自然科学基金委与日本学术振兴会（JSPS）学术交流项目的支持。双方分享了缪子物理实验的最新进展，并在上海交通大学闵行校区GIMLID Pizza共进午餐。李亮教授、许金祥教授、黄冠铭、吕濛、王江涛、曾永浩和Zejia Lu参加了午餐学术交流。
