PPC 2023: XVI International Conference on Interactions between Particle Physics and Cosmology

Kim Siang recently took part in the PPC 2023 conference (Jun 12 – 16), held at the Institute for Basic Science (IBS) in Daejeon, South Korea. Representing the Fermilab Muon g-2 Collaboration, he gave a talk on the “Muon g-2 experiment” in the Flavor Physics plenary session. For more details, please visit the event webpage: https://indico.ibs.re.kr/event/540/. After four years away from in-person international conferences, it was rewarding to take part in one again. The occasion was all the more special as it marked Kim Siang’s first return to South Korea since 2006.
