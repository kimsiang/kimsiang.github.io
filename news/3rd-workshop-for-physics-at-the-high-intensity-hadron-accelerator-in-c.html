3rd workshop for “Physics at the high-intensity hadron accelerator in China”

Kim Siang presented “Progress in Precision Muon Physics” at the 3rd Workshop on “Physics at the High-Intensity Hadron Accelerator in China”, held on 7–8 December 2019 at the China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS) in Dongguan. More information can be found here: https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/10779/.
