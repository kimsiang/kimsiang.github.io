Global Challenge Program 2026: exchange at Universiti Malaya on muography for urban safety2026“全球挑战计划”：在马来亚大学交流缪子成像在城市安全中的应用

In Kuala Lumpur, the team visited the Department of Physics at Universiti Malaya (UM). Team members gave talks and detector demonstrations, and the discussion turned to how muon physics can support urban safety. Kuala Lumpur City Hall (DBKL) and Prasarana, the national public-transport infrastructure company, joined the discussion on applications.

With support from DBKL and Prasarana, the team also ran preliminary tests and a site survey around Masjid Jamek station, near the area of the Jalan Masjid India road collapse, looking for potential voids and loose ground. Preliminary work with Prasarana on metro-tunnel safety has since begun, and the UM Department of Physics expressed interest in joint student training with TDLI.

This visit was part of the group’s Global Challenge Program 2026 trip across Peninsular Malaysia (4–11 August). Read the full trip summary, or the report in Chinese on SJTU News.

在吉隆坡，团队走访马来亚大学（UM）物理系。团队成员作了报告并进行探测器演示，双方围绕缪子物理在城市安全中的应用展开讨论。吉隆坡市政厅（DBKL）和马来西亚国家基建公司（Prasarana）也参与了应用讨论。

在DBKL和Prasarana的支持下，团队还在印度清真寺路（Jalan Masjid India）道路塌陷区域附近的占美清真寺（Masjid Jamek）站周边开展初步测试和现场勘察，排查潜在空洞与松散地层。目前，课题组已与Prasarana就地铁隧道安全开展前期合作；马来亚大学物理系也表达了与李政道研究所联合培养学生的意愿。

本次交流是课题组2026“全球挑战计划”马来西亚半岛之行（8月4日至11日）的一站。查看行程总结，或阅读上海交通大学新闻网的相关报道。
