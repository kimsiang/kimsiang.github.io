National Physics Conference of Malaysia (PERFIK 2024)2024年马来西亚全国物理学大会（PERFIK 2024）

Hoh Siew Yan recently participated in the National Physics Conference 2024 (PERFIK 2024), held in Malaysia from November 3 to 8, 2024. Representing the group, Hoh gave two presentations highlighting the team’s ongoing work in both fundamental research and educational outreach.

In the Particle Physics category, Hoh’s presentation, “Precision Measurement of the Muon’s Electric and Magnetic Dipole Moments”, focused on two key experiments: the Muon g-2 Experiment at Fermilab and the Muon Electric Dipole Moment (muEDM) Experiment at PSI. The talk reviewed the g-2 Experiment, which has measured the muon’s anomalous magnetic moment with unprecedented precision, and its implications for physics beyond the Standard Model. Hoh also discussed the muEDM Experiment, currently in its commissioning phase, and its potential to probe charge-parity violation through a highly sensitive search for the muon’s electric dipole moment.

In the Physics Education category, Hoh presented “From Clouds to Sparks: Scintillating Cosmic Rays in the Classroom”, which showcased the group’s efforts to bring cosmic-ray physics into the classroom. Using interactive tools such as cloud chambers, spark chambers, and scintillation detectors, Hoh demonstrated how complex concepts can be made accessible to students, underscoring the value of hands-on experiments in inspiring curiosity and deepening learners’ understanding of high-energy physics.

PERFIK is an annual conference organized by Institut Fizik Malaysia (IFM), the sole representative body for physicists and those engaged in physics-related work in Malaysia. IFM promotes physics research, fosters collaboration in education, training, and practice, and works to elevate the professional status of physicists. Hoh’s presentations drew considerable attention and lively discussion, reflecting strong interest in both the technical and educational sides of the group’s work. More details about the conference can be found at https://perfik.ifm.org.my/.

近日，Hoh Siew Yan 参加了2024年11月3日至8日在马来西亚举行的2024年马来西亚全国物理学大会（PERFIK 2024）。他代表课题组作了两场报告，展示了团队在基础研究和教育科普两方面正在开展的工作。

在粒子物理分会上，Hoh 作了题为“缪子电偶极矩与磁偶极矩的精密测量”的报告，重点介绍了两项关键实验：美国费米国家加速器实验室（Fermilab）缪子 g-2 实验和瑞士保罗谢勒研究所（PSI）缪子电偶极矩（muEDM）实验。报告回顾了以前所未有的精度测量缪子反常磁矩的 g-2 实验，及其对超越标准模型新物理的意义；还介绍了目前处于调试阶段的 muEDM 实验，以及该实验通过高灵敏度寻找缪子电偶极矩来探测电荷—宇称（CP）破坏的潜力。

在物理教育分会上，Hoh 作了题为“从云雾到火花：课堂中的闪烁宇宙线”的报告，展示了课题组将宇宙线物理引入课堂的努力。借助云室、火花室和闪烁探测器等互动教具，他演示了如何让学生理解复杂的物理概念，强调了动手实验在激发好奇心、加深学习者对高能物理理解方面的价值。

PERFIK 是马来西亚物理学会（Institut Fizik Malaysia，IFM）每年主办的学术会议。IFM 是马来西亚物理学家及物理相关从业者唯一的代表性机构，致力于推动物理学研究，促进教育、培训与实践领域的合作，并提升物理学家的职业地位。Hoh 的报告引起了广泛关注和热烈讨论，体现出与会者对课题组在技术与教育两方面工作的浓厚兴趣。更多会议详情请见 https://perfik.ifm.org.my/。
