The 3rd National Muography Workshop第三届全国缪子成像技术与应用研讨会

Our group recently participated in the 3rd National Muography Workshop (第三届全国缪子成像技术与应用研讨会), held at the University of South China (USC) in Hengyang (衡阳) from November 22 to 23, 2025. Representing our team, Yinghe Wang presented the latest progress of the SJTU Muography Project. Siew Yan Hoh, Min Yang Tan, and Jiangtao Wang also presented posters on the progress of the Shanghai Outer Ring Tunnel Muography project and the SHINE Muon Tomograph project. The workshop brought together researchers from institutes and universities across China to exchange insights on the latest developments in muography technologies and their applications, providing a platform for experts in muon detection, electronics, and imaging algorithms to foster collaborations, explore new ideas, and establish new research directions. During the Chinese Muography Collaboration Committee Meeting, bids to host the 5th edition were considered. I am delighted to announce that our institute has won the bid to host the 5th National Muography Workshop in autumn 2027! Recordings of the talks can be found here: https://www.koushare.com/live/details/48437.

2025年11月22日至23日，第三届全国缪子成像技术与应用研讨会在衡阳南华大学举行，课题组成员参加了此次会议。王赢鹤代表团队报告了上海交通大学缪子成像项目的最新进展；何守仁、陈铭扬和王江涛则以墙报形式分别介绍了上海外环隧道缪子成像项目和SHINE缪子成像装置项目的进展。研讨会汇聚了全国各研究所和高校的研究人员，围绕缪子成像技术及其应用的最新进展开展交流，为缪子探测、电子学和成像算法等领域的专家搭建了促进合作、探索新思路、开拓新研究方向的平台。在中国缪子成像合作委员会会议上，与会代表审议了第五届研讨会的申办事宜。我很高兴地宣布，我们研究所成功获得2027年秋季第五届全国缪子成像技术与应用研讨会的主办权！会议报告录像请见：https://www.koushare.com/live/details/48437。
