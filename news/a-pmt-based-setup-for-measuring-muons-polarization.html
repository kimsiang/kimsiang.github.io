A PMT-based setup for measuring muon polarization基于光电倍增管的缪子极化测量装置

In addition to the setup designed and built by the ZIRC team, we have another version of the muon spin polarization measurement setup, based on PMTs and plastic scintillators.

除了致远创新研究中心（ZIRC）团队设计搭建的装置外，我们还有另一版基于光电倍增管（PMT）和塑料闪烁体的缪子自旋极化测量装置。
