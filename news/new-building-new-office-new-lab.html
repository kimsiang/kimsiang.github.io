New building, new office, new lab!新大楼、新办公室、新实验室！

We have completed our move to a new building (李政道研究所张江实验楼) in Zhangjiang, Pudong New District! We look forward to working in the new office and the new lab (to be renovated) for many years to come. More pictures below (credit to my colleagues who took these nice pictures!).

课题组已顺利搬入位于浦东新区张江的新大楼（李政道研究所张江实验楼）！期待在新办公室和新实验室（待装修）里开启未来多年的工作。更多照片见下方（感谢同事们拍摄的精美照片！）。
