ICHEP 2024: 42nd International Conference on High Energy PhysicsICHEP 2024：第42届国际高能物理大会

Kim Siang recently participated in the ICHEP 2024 conference (July 17–24) at the Prague Congress Centre in Prague, Czech Republic. Representing the Fermilab Muon g-2 collaboration, he gave a talk in the Quark and Lepton Flavour Physics parallel session on the “Run-2/3 measurement of the muon anomalous magnetic moment by the Muon g-2 experiment at Fermilab”. He also presented a poster on the “Beam dynamics corrections to measurements of the muon anomalous magnetic moment”. It was a very fruitful trip: he learned about the latest developments in the field, picked up practical knowledge on detector development and scientific outreach, and, more importantly, reconnected with old friends. For more details, please visit the event’s webpage: https://indico.cern.ch/event/1291157/overview.

许金祥近日参加了在捷克布拉格会议中心举行的ICHEP 2024第42届国际高能物理大会（7月17日至24日）。他代表费米实验室Muon g-2合作组在夸克与轻子味物理分会场作了题为“费米实验室Muon g-2实验Run-2/3缪子反常磁矩测量”的报告，并展示了题为“缪子反常磁矩测量中的束流动力学修正”的墙报。此行收获颇丰：他了解了领域最新进展，汲取了探测器研制与科学传播方面的实用经验，更重要的是与老朋友再度相聚。会议详情请见：https://indico.cern.ch/event/1291157/overview。
