Pujiang Innovation Forum 2024浦江创新论坛2024

On 7 September, the 2024 Pujiang Innovation Forum (the 17th) opened at the Zhangjiang Science Hall in Shanghai and ran until 10 September. The theme of the forum was “Shared Innovation, Shaping the Future Together: Building an Open Environment for Scientific and Technological Innovation”. Hungarian President Tamás Sulyok, representing the guest country of honour, sent a congratulatory letter. Chen Jining, Secretary of the Shanghai Municipal Party Committee, attended the opening ceremony and gave a speech; Wan Gang, President of the China Association for Science and Technology, and Yin Hejun, Minister of Science and Technology, delivered keynote speeches. Gong Zheng, Deputy Secretary of the Shanghai Municipal Party Committee and Mayor of Shanghai, chaired the opening ceremony. The forum brought together more than 300 experts and scholars from international organisations, leading universities, research institutions and industry to discuss how to build an open, fair, just and non-discriminatory environment for scientific and technological innovation. Kim Siang Khaw took part in several sessions of the 2024 Pujiang Innovation Forum.

In the “Young Scholars Special Lecture” session of the main forum, Kim Siang gave a talk entitled “The Heavy Electron: Exploring the Unknowns of the Microscopic and Macroscopic Worlds with Muons”, introducing how muons can be used to explore the macroscopic and microscopic worlds and “see through” heaven and earth. He was also invited to the “Shanghai Internet Influencers Salon” on 9 September, where, together with Xu Jiong, Director of the Cyberspace Affairs Office of the Shanghai Municipal Party Committee; Xu Feng, Party Secretary of the Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Work Committee; Yang Jun, Deputy Director and First-level Inspector of the Cyberspace Affairs Office; Shi Qian, Party Group Member and Vice President of the Shanghai Association for Science and Technology; and Chen Hongkai, Second-level Inspector of the Science and Technology Commission of Shanghai Municipality, he shared views on hot online topics around building Shanghai into a science and technology innovation centre. Under the title “A Journey into the Mysteries of the Muon: Integrating Research, Science Communication and Innovation”, Kim Siang described a path spanning basic research, public engagement and technological innovation: by combining research with science communication, one can both explore the mysteries of the universe and contribute to a thriving innovation ecosystem. He also shared a reflection from his visit to the Qian Xuesen Library: science communication is not only about spreading knowledge, but also about promoting the scientific method, scientific ethics and the scientific spirit.

9月7日，2024浦江创新论坛（第十七届）在上海张江科学会堂开幕，并持续至9月10日。论坛主题为“共享创新 共塑未来：构建科技创新开放环境”。主宾国匈牙利总统舒尤克·道马什向论坛致贺信。上海市委书记陈吉宁出席开幕式并致辞，中国科学技术协会主席万钢、科技部部长阴和俊作主旨演讲。上海市委副书记、市长龚正主持开幕式。本届论坛邀请了300余位来自国际组织、顶尖高校、科研机构和产业界的专家与学者，共同探讨如何更好地打造开放、公平、公正、非歧视的科技创新环境。许金祥参加了2024浦江创新论坛的多个环节。

许金祥在主论坛的“青年特别讲席”环节演讲，演讲主题为《大号的电子-通过缪子探索微观和宏观世界的未知》，介绍如何借助缪子探索宏观与微观世界，“看穿”天地间。此外，许金祥还受邀参加了9月9日的“上海网络大V沙龙”，与市委网信办主任徐炯，市科技工作党委书记徐枫，市委网信办副主任、一级巡视员杨俊，市科协党组成员、副主席石谦，市科委二级巡视员陈宏凯等一起，分享上海科创中心建设的互联网热议话题。许金祥以“缪子探秘之旅：科研、科普与科创的融合之路”为题，介绍了一段贯穿基础研究、公众传播与科技创新的旅程：通过科研与科普的有机结合，既探索宇宙的奥秘，也为科创生态的繁荣贡献力量。他还分享了自己在钱学森图书馆获得的感悟：科普不只是传播知识，更是科学方法、科学道德以及科学精神的弘扬。
