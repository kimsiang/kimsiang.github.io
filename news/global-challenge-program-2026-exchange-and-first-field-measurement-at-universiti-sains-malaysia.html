Global Challenge Program 2026: exchange and first field measurement at Universiti Sains Malaysia2026“全球挑战计划”：在马来西亚理科大学交流并开展首次实地测量

In Penang, the team visited Universiti Sains Malaysia (USM) for academic talks and a technical exchange on cosmic-ray physics, muon detection, data acquisition and image analysis. USM has long experience in geophysics and environmental monitoring, which complements muon imaging well.

The visit also included the trip’s first field measurement: the team set up its portable muon detectors in an air-raid shelter on the USM campus. It was the group’s first demonstration measurement in a real building in Malaysia, used to test detector deployment, data taking and site conditions before the campaigns in Ipoh and Kuala Lumpur. USM and the group agreed to pursue joint research and to explore further applications together.

This visit was part of the group’s Global Challenge Program 2026 trip across Peninsular Malaysia (4–11 August). Read the full trip summary, or the report in Chinese on SJTU News.

在槟城，团队走访马来西亚理科大学（USM），围绕宇宙线物理、缪子探测、数据采集与图像分析作学术报告并开展技术交流。USM在地球物理与环境监测领域积淀深厚，与缪子成像技术形成良好互补。

此行的首次实地测量也在这里进行：团队在USM校内的防空洞中部署了便携式缪子探测器。这是课题组在马来西亚真实建筑中开展的首次演示性测量，用于在怡保和吉隆坡的测量之前检验探测器部署、数据采集及现场条件。双方就开展联合研究、拓展应用场景达成了初步共识。

本次交流是课题组2026“全球挑战计划”马来西亚半岛之行（8月4日至11日）的一站。查看行程总结，或阅读上海交通大学新闻网的相关报道。
