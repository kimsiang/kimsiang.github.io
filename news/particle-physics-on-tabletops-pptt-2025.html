Particle Physics on Tabletops (PPTT 2025)桌面粒子物理研讨会（PPTT 2025）

Members of our group recently participated in the PPTT 2025 workshop, held in Dujiangyan, Chengdu, from 1 to 6 September 2025. The workshop, on Particle Physics on Tabletops, brought together experts from diverse fields to explore the potential of probing fundamental particle physics with tabletop experiments, and to promote cross-disciplinary and international collaboration in this emerging area.

Beining Rao and Guan Ming Wong presented posters at the workshop. Beining presented on “Testing QED and Beyond with ortho-Positronium”, while Guan Ming shared the latest update on the “Development of the Muon Trigger Detector Prototype for the muEDM experiment at PSI”.

Details of the workshop can be found here.

近日，课题组成员参加了2025年9月1日至6日在成都都江堰举行的 PPTT 2025 研讨会。本次“桌面粒子物理”（Particle Physics on Tabletops）研讨会汇聚了来自不同领域的专家，共同探讨利用桌面实验探索基础粒子物理的潜力，推动这一新兴领域的跨学科与国际合作。

饶倍宁和黄冠铭在会上进行了墙报展示。饶倍宁展示了题为“利用正交正电子偶素检验 QED 及超越 QED 的物理”的工作，黄冠铭则介绍了“PSI muEDM 实验缪子触发探测器原型机研制”的最新进展。

研讨会详情请见此处。
