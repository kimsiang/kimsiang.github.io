Global Challenge Program 2026: exchange and detector demonstration at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia2026“全球挑战计划”：在马来西亚理工大学交流并演示探测器

The last university stop of the trip was Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) in Johor. Team members gave talks on cosmic-ray physics, muon detection, data acquisition and image analysis, and demonstrated the group’s portable muon-imaging detectors.

Besides UTM researchers, representatives of the Atomic Energy Licensing Board and a highway company joined the discussions, which ranged from the physics of the measurement to regulatory questions and practical use on transport infrastructure. UTM and the group agreed to pursue joint research and to explore further applications together.

This visit was part of the group’s Global Challenge Program 2026 trip across Peninsular Malaysia (4–11 August). Read the full trip summary, or the report in Chinese on SJTU News.

此行最后一所走访的高校是位于柔佛的马来西亚理工大学（UTM）。团队成员围绕宇宙线物理、缪子探测、数据采集与图像分析作报告，并演示了课题组的便携式缪子成像探测器。

除UTM科研人员外，马来西亚原子能执照局及一家高速公路公司的代表也参与了交流，话题从测量原理延伸到监管问题和交通基础设施中的实际应用。双方就开展联合研究、拓展应用场景达成了初步共识。

本次交流是课题组2026“全球挑战计划”马来西亚半岛之行（8月4日至11日）的一站。查看行程总结，或阅读上海交通大学新闻网的相关报道。
