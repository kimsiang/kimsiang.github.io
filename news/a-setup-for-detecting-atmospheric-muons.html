A setup for detecting atmospheric muons大气缪子探测装置投入运行

After months of hard work by the ZIRC project team, a setup for measuring the polarization of atmospheric muons is now in operation! We look forward to extracting important information for subsequent studies of muon polarization.

经过 ZIRC 项目团队数月的努力，大气缪子极化测量装置现已投入运行！我们期待从中获得重要信息，为后续的缪子极化研究奠定基础。
