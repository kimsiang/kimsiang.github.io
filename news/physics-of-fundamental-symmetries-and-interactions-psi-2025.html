Physics of Fundamental Symmetries and Interactions (PSI 2025)

Beining Rao from our group recently participated in the Physics of Fundamental Symmetries and Interactions – PSI2025 workshop (September 7–12, 2025, Paul Scherrer Institute, Switzerland). The workshop highlights current and upcoming high-precision, low-energy physics initiatives that make use of PSI’s unique facilities, including the world’s most powerful proton cyclotron, high-intensity low-momentum muon beams, and an ultracold neutron source, as well as complementary experimental approaches. Beining presented a poster on her work, titled “Testing QED and Beyond with ortho-Positronium.”
