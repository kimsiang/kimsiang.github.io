Tsung-Dao Lee Frontier Lecture and Special Seminar on Muon g-2

Kim-Siang Khaw gave a T.D. Lee Frontier Lecture and a Special Seminar on muon g-2. The first results from the Muon g-2 Collaboration have generated great interest in the scientific community, and the lecture and seminar together lasted almost four hours.
