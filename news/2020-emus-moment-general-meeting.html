2020.12.11News新闻

2020 EMuS & MOMENT General Meeting2020年EMuS与MOMENT全体会议

Kim-Siang gave a talk on muon g-2/EDM experiments and their requirements for future muon sources at the 2020 EMuS & MOMENT General Meeting (December 11–12, 2020). The meeting is a first step towards fostering collaboration among Chinese institutions in muon physics. For more information, please visit https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/13295.

许金祥在2020年EMuS & MOMENT全体会议（2020年12月11日至12日）上作了关于缪子g-2/EDM实验及其对未来缪子源需求的报告。此次会议是推动国内各单位在缪子物理领域开展合作的第一步。更多信息请访问https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/13295。
