Workshop on Muon Physics at the Intensity and Precision Frontiers (MIP2024)强度与精度前沿缪子物理研讨会（MIP2024）

Our group recently participated in the MIP2024 workshop at Peking University, Beijing, on April 20–21, 2024. Kim Siang chaired the precision measurement session, while three group members (Yusuke, Siew Yan, and Guan Ming) gave plenary talks and nine presented posters. Fangchao, Jun Kai, and Yuzhi won Best Poster Awards for their work on the Shanghai Muon Source, the Muon g-2 experiment, and the muEDM experiment, respectively. Congratulations to them! For more information about the workshop, please visit https://indico.cern.ch/event/1356341/. The next edition, MIP2025, will be hosted by Hunan University in Changsha, and we are all looking forward to it!

2024年4月20日至21日，课题组参加了在北京大学举行的强度与精度前沿缪子物理研讨会（MIP2024）。许金祥主持了精密测量分会，竹内 佑甫、何守仁和黄冠铭三位成员作了大会报告，另有九位成员展示了墙报。刘方超、黄俊凯和尚雨芝分别凭借上海缪子源、Muon g-2实验和muEDM实验相关工作荣获最佳墙报奖，祝贺他们！研讨会详情请见https://indico.cern.ch/event/1356341/。下一届MIP2025将由湖南大学在长沙承办，我们满怀期待！
