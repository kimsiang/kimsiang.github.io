Workshop on Muon Physics at the Intensity and Precision Frontiers强度与精度前沿缪子物理研讨会

The MIP 2023 workshop (April 15–16, 2023) offers a welcoming environment for physicists interested in muon physics and its applications, bringing together the experimental and theoretical communities to share their latest findings. The workshop aims to facilitate discussion of ongoing and proposed experiments and to encourage participants to consider future possibilities.

Designed to promote thoughtful conversation and the exchange of ideas, the workshop offers a fresh perspective on muon-based experiments, and its collaborative atmosphere gives every attendee the opportunity to contribute to and learn from their peers.

Hosted at the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute (TDLI) of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, MIP 2023 is an on-site event open to all who share a passion for muon physics. Join us at TDLI to connect with fellow enthusiasts and deepen your understanding of this fascinating field. The workshop will cover the following topics:

  • Development of muon sources (MELODY, HIAF/CiADS, SMS, MuSIC)
  • Precision measurements (g-2, EDM)
  • Searches for rare processes (MACE, Mu2e, COMET)
  • Muon applications (muSR, muography)
  • Theoretical muon physics
  • Muon accelerators and colliders
  • Neutrino physics

The workshop is co-organized by the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute and the School of Physics and Astronomy of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, the Institute of High Energy Physics CAS, the Institute of Modern Physics CAS, Advanced Energy Science and Technology Guangdong Laboratory, and Sun Yat-Sen University.

MIP 2023研讨会（2023年4月15日至16日）为关注缪子物理及其应用的物理学者提供了开放友好的交流平台，汇聚实验与理论两方面的研究人员分享最新成果。研讨会旨在促进对在建及拟议实验的讨论，并鼓励与会者展望未来的发展方向。

研讨会以促进深入对话和思想交流为宗旨，为缪子相关实验提供新的视角；其合作开放的氛围让每位参会者都有机会贡献所长、相互学习。

MIP 2023由李政道研究所（上海交通大学）主办，为线下会议，欢迎所有热爱缪子物理的同仁参加。欢迎来到李政道研究所，与同道交流，深入了解这一引人入胜的领域。研讨会将涵盖以下主题：

  • 缪子源的发展（MELODY、HIAF/CiADS、SMS、MuSIC）
  • 精密测量（g-2、EDM）
  • 稀有过程寻找（MACE、Mu2e、COMET）
  • 缪子应用（muSR、缪子成像）
  • 缪子理论物理
  • 缪子加速器与对撞机
  • 中微子物理

研讨会由李政道研究所、物理与天文学院（上海交通大学）、中国科学院高能物理研究所、中国科学院近代物理研究所、先进能源科学与技术广东省实验室以及中山大学联合主办。
