Global Challenge Program 2026: popular-science lecture at Chung Ling High School, Penang2026“全球挑战计划”：在槟城钟灵中学举办科普讲座

The first stop of the group’s Global Challenge Program 2026 trip to Malaysia was Chung Ling High School in Penang — Kim Siang Khaw’s alma mater. Returning as an alumnus, Kim Siang gave the students a popular-science lecture on cosmic-ray muons: where they come from, how they are detected, and how they can be used to see inside large structures.

Two SJTU students then spoke about their own paths. Qee Enn Tai, an international student at SJTU, described studying and growing up in Shanghai, and undergraduate Yifei Li shared what it is like to move from the classroom into research. The lecture opened the academic exchanges of the trip, which went on to universities in Penang, Kuala Lumpur and Johor.

This visit was part of the group’s Global Challenge Program 2026 trip across Peninsular Malaysia (4–11 August). Read the full trip summary, or the report in Chinese on SJTU News.

课题组2026“全球挑战计划”马来西亚之行的第一站，是许金祥的母校——槟城钟灵中学。许金祥以校友身份为中学生们举办科普讲座，介绍宇宙线缪子从何而来、如何探测，以及如何利用缪子“透视”大型结构。

随后，两位交大学生分享了各自的成长经历：留学生戴颀恩（Qee Enn Tai）讲述了在上海交大的学习与生活，本科生李逸飞则分享了从课堂走向科研的体会。这场讲座拉开了此行学术交流的序幕，团队随后前往槟城、吉隆坡和柔佛的多所高校。

本次交流是课题组2026“全球挑战计划”马来西亚半岛之行（8月4日至11日）的一站。查看行程总结，或阅读上海交通大学新闻网的相关报道。
