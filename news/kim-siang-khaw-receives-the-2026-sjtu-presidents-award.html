Kim Siang Khaw receives the 2026 SJTU President’s Award

Kim Siang Khaw received the 2026 Shanghai Jiao Tong University President’s Award (上海交通大学校长奖). The award is the university’s highest honour for spiritual and civic achievement, recognising faculty and staff whose work embodies the SJTU spirit.

The awards were presented on 6 April 2026 at the Huo Yingdong Sports Center on the Minhang Campus, during SJTU’s 2026 Global Alumni Return Day, which also celebrated the university’s 130th anniversary. Ten individuals and teams were honoured this year.
