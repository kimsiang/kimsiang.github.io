International Workshop on Muon Physics at the Intensity and Precision Frontiers (MIP2026)强流与精密前沿缪子物理国际研讨会（MIP2026）

Our group took part in the International Workshop on Muon Physics at the Intensity and Precision Frontiers (MIP2026), held from 24 to 28 April 2026 in Huizhou, Guangdong, and organised by the Institute of Modern Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IMP-CAS). The group gave two talks and three posters covering all four of our research directions.

Talks

  • Yonghao Zeng【曾永浩】 presented the final results of the Fermilab Muon g-2 Experiment, which measured the muon magnetic anomaly to 127 parts per billion, including how the anomalous precession frequency ωa is extracted from the data.
  • Jun Kai Ng【黄俊凯】 gave the talk “Current Status of the SHINE Muon Source” on 25 April, on the high-repetition-rate electron-driven muon source planned at SHINE, developed with the Shanghai Advanced Research Institute (SARI) and ShanghaiTech University.

Posters

  • Guan Ming Wong【黄冠铭】 presented the muon trigger detector (MTD) for the PSI muEDM experiment.
  • Jia Cheng Yap【叶嘉诚】 presented the beam test of the SHINE Muon Source (SMS).
  • Jiangtao Wang【王江涛】 presented accelerator-based muography at SHINE.

MIP is a yearly meeting for the muon physics community in China and beyond; see our reports from previous MIP workshops on the News page.

2026年4月24日至28日，由中国科学院近代物理研究所主办的强流与精密前沿缪子物理国际研讨会（MIP2026）在广东惠州举行，课题组成员参加了此次会议，共作两个口头报告和三个墙报报告，内容涵盖课题组全部四个研究方向。

口头报告

  • 曾永浩报告了费米实验室Muon g-2实验的最终结果——该实验以127 ppb（十亿分之一百二十七）的精度测定了缪子反常磁矩，并介绍了如何从数据中提取反常进动频率ωa。
  • 黄俊凯于4月25日作题为“SHINE缪子源现状”（Current Status of the SHINE Muon Source）的报告，介绍了课题组与中国科学院上海高等研究院、上海科技大学共同推进、拟在SHINE上建设的高重频电子驱动缪子源。

墙报

  • 黄冠铭介绍了PSI muEDM实验的缪子触发探测器（MTD）。
  • 叶嘉诚介绍了SHINE缪子源（SMS）的束流测试。
  • 王江涛介绍了基于SHINE加速器的缪子成像。

MIP是面向国内外缪子物理学界的年度会议；往届MIP研讨会的报道请见新闻页面中的往期MIP研讨会。
