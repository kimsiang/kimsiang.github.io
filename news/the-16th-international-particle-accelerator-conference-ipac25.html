The 16th International Particle Accelerator Conference (IPAC’25)第十六届国际粒子加速器会议（IPAC’25）

Dr. Yusuke Takeuchi recently attended the 16th International Particle Accelerator Conference (IPAC’25), held in Taipei from June 1 to 6, 2025. IPAC is the largest conference in the accelerator field, with a total of 1,000 participants this year. He presented a poster on muon source design studies titled “Design Studies on A kHz–MHz Repetition Rate Pulsed Muon Source Based on Electron Accelerator.” For more information about the conference, please visit https://ipac25.org/.

“I had meaningful discussions with many experts and learned about the latest research trends in accelerator physics. I was impressed by the momentum of studies for future lepton colliders, including the Muon Collider, and by the efforts on advanced beam dynamics simulation tools (Xsuite now has a spin-tracking feature).” – Yusuke Takeuchi

竹内佑甫博士近日参加了2025年6月1日至6日在台北举行的第十六届国际粒子加速器会议（IPAC’25）。IPAC是加速器领域规模最大的国际会议，本届参会人数达1000人。他展示了关于缪子源设计研究的墙报，题为“基于电子加速器的kHz–MHz重复频率脉冲缪子源设计研究”。会议详情请访问https://ipac25.org/。

“我与许多专家进行了富有意义的交流，了解了加速器物理的最新研究动态。未来轻子对撞机（包括缪子对撞机）研究的蓬勃势头，以及先进束流动力学模拟工具的发展（Xsuite现已具备自旋追踪功能），都给我留下了深刻印象。”——竹内佑甫
