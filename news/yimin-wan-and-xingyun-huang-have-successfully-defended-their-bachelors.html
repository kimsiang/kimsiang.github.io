Yimin Wan and Xingyun Huang have successfully defended their Bachelor’s theses

Yimin Wan (万一民) and Xingyun Huang (黄幸运) successfully defended their Bachelor’s theses on 4 June 2024. They worked on “Design and Construction of Spark Chamber Frame Structure, Gas System, and Camera System” and “Spark-Chamber-based Muon Mass Measurement”. The whole team congratulates them! Xingyun will stay in our group as a PhD student working on the PSI muEDM project, and Yimin will join the Shanghai Advanced Research Institute (SARI) as a Master’s student in Microelectronics and Solid-State Electronics.
