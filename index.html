01About the group关于课题组

“Who ordered that?”

— I. I. Rabi, on the muon谈缪子

5Countries & regions国家/地区

Although the muon was discovered back in 1936, it remains at the heart of frontier research. Our group pursues precision-frontier experiments with the muon as a probe, measuring its fundamental properties — the anomalous magnetic moment (g−2) and the electric dipole moment (EDM) — to test and challenge the Standard Model of particle physics. 尽管缪子早在1936年就被发现，但至今仍处于前沿研究的核心。我们课题组致力于以缪子为探针的精密前沿实验，精确测量缪子的基本性质——包括磁异常矩(g-2)和电偶极矩(EDM)——以检验和挑战粒子物理标准模型。

We also develop muon beams, detectors, simulation and data analysis, and drive two flagship projects: China's first high-repetition-rate pulsed muon source at the SHINE facility, and muon imaging (muography) for exploring urban underground space. 同时，我们积极开展缪子束流开发、探测器研制、模拟仿真与数据分析，并推动两大重点项目：依托SHINE装置建设中国首个高重复频率脉冲缪子源，以及面向城市地下空间探测的缪子成像技术。

P-01Fermilab · USA

Muon g−2

Anomalous magnetic moment缪子反常磁矩

Precision goal精度目标
140 ppb
Experiment实验
Fermilab E989
Our role我们的工作
ωa analysis · beam dynamicsωa测量 · 束流动力学修正

Fermilab's E989 aims to measure the muon g−2 to 140 ppb. If the gap between theory and experiment holds, the significance would exceed 7σ — a clear sign of physics beyond the Standard Model. We are responsible for the anomalous precession frequency measurement and beam-dynamics corrections.费米实验室E989实验致力于将缪子g-2测量精度提升至140 ppb。若理论与实验的偏差保持不变，统计显著性将超过7σ——明确的超越标准模型新物理信号。课题组负责反常进动频率测量及束流动力学修正。

P-02PSI · Switzerland

muEDM

Muon electric dipole moment缪子电偶极矩

Technique技术
Frozen-spin冻结自旋
Sensitivity目标灵敏度
10−23–10−22 e·cm
Our role我们的工作
Muon entrance trigger detector缪子入射触发探测器

A permanent EDM of any fundamental particle would signal CP violation, helping explain the universe's matter–antimatter asymmetry. At PSI we use the frozen-spin technique, targeting 10⁻²³–10⁻²² e·cm. Our group develops the muon entrance trigger detector.任何基本粒子的永久电偶极矩都意味着CP对称性破坏，有助于解释宇宙物质-反物质不对称。我们在PSI采用冻结自旋技术，目标灵敏度达10⁻²³–10⁻²² e·cm。课题组负责缪子入射触发探测器研发。

China's first e⁻-driven muon source中国首个电子驱动缪子源

Using the high-repetition pulsed electron beam (up to MHz) of SHINE's 8 GeV superconducting RF linac, we are building China's first electron-driven muon source downstream of the beam dump — opening new experiments in condensed matter, materials science and particle physics.利用上海SHINE装置8 GeV超导射频直线加速器的高重频脉冲电子束（可达MHz量级），在beam dump下游建设中国首个电子驱动缪子源，为凝聚态、材料科学和粒子物理开辟全新实验可能。

P-04Shanghai field sites

Muography

Muon imaging缪子成像

Detectors探测器
Scintillator + SiPM trackers塑料闪烁体 + SiPM径迹探测器
Field sites现场验证
Shanghai Outer Ring Tunnel上海外环隧道
Output成果
Non-destructive density imaging无损密度成像

Cosmic-ray muons penetrate deep into matter, letting us image underground structures without damage. Since mid-2024 we have built our own muography detectors; a pilot run in Shanghai's Outer Ring Tunnel showed muon flux tracking tidal water level and sediment overburden.利用宇宙射线缪子的强穿透能力，对地下结构进行无损成像。自2024年年中起，我们自主研制缪子成像探测器；在上海外环隧道开展的先导实验表明，缪子通量可灵敏反映潮位与泥沙覆盖层的变化。

07Teaching & training教学与培训

Training the next generation教育与人才培养

From classroom to laboratory, undergraduate to graduate, we train the next generation of particle-physics and detector-technology talent.从课堂到实验室，从本科生到研究生，我们致力于培养下一代粒子物理与探测器技术人才。

  1. PHY1950

    Introduction to Physics Research物理研究导论

    For first-year Zhiyuan College undergraduates: research methods, Fermi estimates, literature search, paper writing and presentations.面向致远学院一年级本科生：科学研究方法、费米估算、文献检索、论文写作与学术报告。

  2. PHY6009

    Scientific Writing & Research Ethics科学写作与学术伦理

    For graduate students (year 2+): training in English scientific writing and academic integrity.面向二年级及以上研究生：英文科学论文写作训练与学术诚信规范。

  3. AI

    Introduction to AIAI入门课程

    AI fundamentals for physics students, exploring frontier applications of AI in particle physics.面向物理学生的人工智能基础，探索AI在粒子物理研究中的前沿应用。

  4. GEANT4

    From Track to Truth: Geant4 muon simulation从径迹到真相：Geant4缪子模拟 ↗

    A free, bilingual 12-week online course — from a 50-line Python Monte Carlo to a virtual experiment students can defend, with hands-on labs, 3D event displays and quizzes.免费的12周中英双语在线课程——从50行Python蒙特卡罗小程序起步，直到完成可答辩的虚拟实验，含动手实验、三维事例显示与自测题。

  5. SUMMER

    International summer internship暑期国际实习

    1–2 months of immersive training: particle-physics basics, detectors and DAQ, simulation and analysis, tabletop muon experiments.1-2个月沉浸式培训：粒子物理入门、探测器与DAQ系统、模拟分析、缪子桌面实验。

  6. STEM

    CosmicWatch cosmic-ray detectorCosmic Watch宇宙射线探测器

    An open-source, desktop-scale muon detector kit for STEM education and public outreach.基于开源设计的桌面级缪子探测器套件，面向STEM教育与科学普及。

09Timeline发展历程

Our journey我们的旅程

  1. 2019

    Kim joins TDLIKim加入TDLI

    Kim Siang Khaw joins the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute as a T.D. Lee Fellow and begins building the muon physics group.许金祥以T.D. Lee Fellow身份加入李政道研究所，开启缪子物理课题组筹建。

  2. 2020

    Group founded课题组正式成立

    Formally joins the Muon g−2 and muEDM international collaborations. First students join.正式加入Muon g-2和muEDM两大国际合作实验。首批学生入组。

  3. 2021

    Growing team团队壮大

    First postdoc and several graduate students join. Muon journal club and Tea Time seminars launched.首位博士后和多名研究生加入。启动缪子Journal Club和Tea Time学术活动。

  4. 2022

    Detector lab opens探测器实验室建成

    Muon detector lab renovation completed. First muon entrance detector built and shipped to PSI for a muEDM beam test in November.缪子探测器实验室装修完成。首个缪子入射探测器研制完成，并于11月运往PSI参加muEDM束流测试。

  5. 2023

    Muon polarimetry & SHINE muon source缪子极化测量与SHINE缪子源

    Atmospheric-muon polarization setups built with ZIRC students come online. Shanghai Muon Source design presented at IPAC'23 and MELODY 2023.与致远创新研究中心学生合作搭建的大气缪子极化测量装置投入运行。上海缪子源设计方案在IPAC'23与MELODY 2023上报告。

  6. 2024

    Muography begins启动缪子成像研究

    From mid-2024 the group takes up muography: first detector shown to the public in July and the Shanghai Muography Project launched. Second muEDM trigger-detector beam test at PSI.2024年年中起课题组开展缪子成像研究：7月首台探测器向公众展示，启动上海缪子成像项目。在PSI完成第二次muEDM触发探测器束流测试。

  7. 2025

    SMS 2025 · TenureSMS 2025 · Kim获长聘

    Hosted SMS 2025, the first SHINE muon source workshop. Outer Ring Tunnel muography paper published and Cosmic-ray Muography System online. Kim receives tenure.主办首届SHINE缪子源及其应用研讨会（SMS 2025）。外环隧道缪子成像论文发表，宇宙射线缪子成像系统（CMS）上线。许金祥获长聘。

  8. 2026

    NuFact 2026 @ TDLI

    Hosted NuFact 2026 at TDLI (31 Aug – 5 Sep), chaired by Kim Siang Khaw — the flagship international workshop on neutrino factories and muon colliders.8月31日至9月5日在李政道研究所主办NuFact 2026，由许金祥担任会议主席——中微子工厂和缪子对撞机领域的旗舰国际会议。
