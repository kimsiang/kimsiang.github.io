Muon Production

Cosmogenic Muons

  • Product of pion and kaon decays
  • Mean Energy : ~ 3 GeV at sea level
  • Flux at sea level : 1 μ/ cm2/ min

Accelerator Muons

  • Energy : A few 100 MeV to 160 GeV
