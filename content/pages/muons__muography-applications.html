Muon Radiography (absorption-based)

Based on The Measurement of The Muon Flux

  • knowledge of the muon energy spectrum
  • calculate the survival rate of muons after passing through the target
  • the average energy loss rate satisfies the Bethe-Bloch formula
  • estimate the density of the target and image it (2D, 3D)

Muon Tomography (scattering-based)

  • hit position and trajectory reconstruction
  • the root mean square (rms) of the projected angular distribution
  • common 3D imaging algorithm: Point of Closest Approach (PoCA)
