Kim Siang participated in the first in-person Physics Week of the Muon g-2 collaboration at Liverpool since he joined the TDLI in 2019. He presented the current status of the precession frequency analysis of the SJTU group, co-organized the precession frequency analysis workshop, and gave a talk about detector acceptance at the beam dynamics workshop. All these were made possible with continuous contributions from Cheng, Yonghao and Jun Kai over the past few months.
