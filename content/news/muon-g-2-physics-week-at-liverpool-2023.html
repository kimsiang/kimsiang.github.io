Kim Siang attended the Muon g-2 collaboration’s Physics Week in Liverpool, his first in-person Physics Week since joining TDLI in 2019. He presented the current status of the SJTU group’s precession frequency analysis, co-organized the precession frequency analysis workshop, and gave a talk on detector acceptance at the beam dynamics workshop. All of this was made possible by the continuous contributions of Cheng, Yonghao, and Jun Kai over the past few months.
