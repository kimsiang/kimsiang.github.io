Kim Siang made his first international business trip in three years, travelling to PSI for the Muon EDM Collaboration April Meeting 2023 on April 15–16. He presented TDLI’s contribution to the collaboration: the muon telescope detector used in the November 2022 beam test. The two-day meeting was very fruitful, with plenty of physics and technical discussions, and delicious food from PSI’s OASE cafeteria.
