Our group recently took part in an exhibition showcasing science and technology (S&T) innovations from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). The exhibition was specially arranged to celebrate the partnership between SJTU and the Shanghai Tech Museum in promoting S&T innovation and public outreach. We took this opportunity to present our newly developed muography detector, with a live LED event display board, to the public; it attracted much attention, especially from younger visitors. We hope to promote this technique for imaging the city’s underground structures to help prevent disasters. Jun Kai, Tianqi, Yingying, and Siew Yan explained the detector to visitors at the museum on July 11–12. Yonghao, Tianqi, Xingyun, Jun Kai, Guan Ming, Ning, Yusuke, and Siew Yan were involved in developing the detector.
