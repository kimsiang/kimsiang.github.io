Our group recently participated in the MIP2025 workshop, held at Hunan University in Changsha from May 17 to 19. Kim Siang chaired the muon bound-state session, while three group members (Yusuke, Guan Ming, and Siew Yan) gave plenary talks on their work on the Shanghai Muon Source, the muEDM experiment, and the Shanghai METRO platform for education, research and outreach, respectively. For more information about the workshop, please visit https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/24109/. The next edition, MIP2026, will be hosted by the Institute of Modern Physics (IMP) in Huizhou, and we are all very excited about it!
