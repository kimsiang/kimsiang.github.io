From 13 July to 23 August 2026, Darwin Lo from Imperial College London joined our group through the SJTU Summer Research Internship Program (Project 161: Fundamental and Applied Muon Physics), supervised by Kim Siang Khaw.

Darwin's project, “Momentum-Informed Angle Statistics Reconstruction for Cosmic-Ray Muon Scattering Tomography”, was a simulation study of how exposure time, muon momentum information, contrast-to-noise ratio and adaptive spatial resolution affect scattering-tomography images. His simulated setup used two pairs of 1 m × 1 m tracking detectors to image a 10 cm tungsten cube embedded in a 40 cm concrete block. Alongside the simulation, he also worked hands-on with a cosmic-ray tracker and its readout electronics in the Muon Tech Lab.

He presented his results at an end-of-project presentation on 21 August 2026. Thank you, Darwin, and all the best for your next steps!
