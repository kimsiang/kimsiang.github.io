Kim Siang was a lecturer at the TDLI & INPAC Winter School in Particle Physics 2025 (January 20–24, 2025, Shanghai). The school was aimed at undergraduate students interested in particle physics who had completed at least one year of university physics. He gave a lecture on muon physics titled “From muon production, muon research to muon applications.” More details about the winter school can be found on the event page.
