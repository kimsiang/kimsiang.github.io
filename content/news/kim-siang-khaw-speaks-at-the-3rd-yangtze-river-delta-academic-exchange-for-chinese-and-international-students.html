From 24 to 27 May 2026, Kim Siang Khaw took part in “Perceiving China” — the 3rd Academic Exchange and Practice Programme for Chinese and International University Students in the Yangtze River Delta (“感知中国”——第三届长三角地区中外大学生学术交流实践活动) in Hangzhou, Zhejiang. Hosted by the China Scholarship Council and co-organised by Zhejiang University under the theme “Smart Yangtze River Delta, Modernising Together” (智汇长三角·同行现代化), the event brought together about 150 Chinese and international students and scholars from 24 universities and 40 countries.

As an invited expert in the plenary session, Kim gave the talk “Crossing Borders, Crossing Disciplines”. Drawing on frontier science and his own path through Malaysia, Japan, Switzerland, the USA and China, he explained why crossing national and disciplinary boundaries matters in modern research, and encouraged young scholars to explore the unknown and build international and interdisciplinary collaborations. The talk was warmly received.

Kim joined as SJTU’s invited mentor, together with three of SJTU’s international students. PhD student Yasin Amara Sekou S Dra won a third prize for best presentation in a parallel session, and Sabir Rasheed and Sien Ping Chew presented posters. Participants also visited Zhejiang University’s Future Learning Center to try out AI agents, agricultural robots and an AI creation workshop.
