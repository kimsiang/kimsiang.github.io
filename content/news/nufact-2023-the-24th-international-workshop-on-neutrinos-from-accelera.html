Our group participated in the NuFact 2023 workshop at Seoul National University, giving technical talks on the Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment (Kim Siang) and the PSI muEDM experiment (Guan Ming). Jun Kai also presented a poster on the November 2022 test beam of the muEDM project. It was great to reconnect with many long-time friends from Japan, Europe, and the US. Besides the rich physics program, the organizers set aside half a day for an excursion in Seoul. We visited the famous Gyeongbokgung (the main royal palace of the Joseon dynasty), the National Folk Museum of Korea (inside Gyeongbokgung), and the National Museum of Korea. The conference banquet was held at Sesangui Modeun Achim on the 50th floor of the FKI Plaza, with a great night view of Seoul. More information about the conference can be found here: http://nufact2023.snu.ac.kr/index.html.
