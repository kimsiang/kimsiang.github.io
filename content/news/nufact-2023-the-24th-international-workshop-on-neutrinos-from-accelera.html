Our group participated in the NuFACT 2023 conference at Seoul National University and presented some technical talks on the Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment (Kim Siang) and the PSI MuEDM experiment (Guan Ming). A poster about the Nov 2022 test beam of the MuEDM project was also presented (Jun Kai). It was great to reconnect with many long-time friends from Japan, Europe and the US. Apart from the rich physics program, the organizer also allocated half a day for an excursion in Seoul. We visited the famous Gyeongbokgung (the main royal palace of the Joseon dynasty), The National Folk Museum of Korea (inside of Gyeongbokgung) and the National Museum of Korea.Conference banquet was held in the FKI Plaza (on the 50th floor) Sesangui Modeun Achim with a great night view of the Seoul city. More information about the conference can be found here: http://nufact2023.snu.ac.kr/index.html.
