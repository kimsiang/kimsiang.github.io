We are accepting students for the SJTU Summer Research Internship this summer! Our project is Fundamental and Applied Muon Physics: measurement of muon properties using tabletop experiments and 3D density imaging using muography detectors.

Eligibility requirements:
– Have completed at least one year of an undergraduate program and be currently enrolled as an undergraduate;
– Hold a GPA of at least 3.0 on a 4.0 scale, or equivalent;
– Students from non-English-speaking countries must provide an English language proficiency certificate.

The program runs from June 30 to August 10, 2025. Applications must be submitted by May 30 to be considered.

More information about the internship (Project 154) can be found in the brochure: https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/en/news/view/718.
