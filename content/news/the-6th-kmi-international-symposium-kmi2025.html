Kim Siang was invited to present the current status of the Fermilab Muon g-2 and PSI muEDM experiments at the 6th KMI International Symposium – “Quest for the Origin of Particles and the Universe” (KMI2025), held from March 5 to 7, 2025, at the Kobayashi-Maskawa Institute of Nagoya University. More information about the symposium can be found here: https://indico.kmi.nagoya-u.ac.jp/event/10/.
