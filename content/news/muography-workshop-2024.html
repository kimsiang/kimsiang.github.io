Kim Siang recently participated in the Muography Workshop 2024, held in Santa Fe, New Mexico, USA, from November 4–8. He presented the latest progress of the Shanghai Muography Project, a collaboration with the Shanghai Urban Planning and Natural Resources Bureau. The workshop brought together researchers from universities and institutes to share their progress, alongside industry representatives who showcased their business strategies and latest products. Beyond advances in muon detection technologies, participants gained valuable insights into geology and archaeology, particularly the history of pyramids around the world. A highlight was K. Borozdin’s timely talk on balancing commercialization and fundamental research in muography, which offered useful guidance as our group navigates this evolving field and strives to integrate both perspectives. More information about the workshop can be found here: 2024 Muography Workshop.
