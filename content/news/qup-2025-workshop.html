Kim Siang and Guan Ming participated in the QUP 2025 workshop, held at KEK, Japan, on April 8–10, 2025. QUP stands for the International Center for Quantum-field Measurement Systems for Studies of the Universe and Particles. This edition of the workshop focused on searching for unknown quantum fields and studying gravity with low-temperature detectors and quantum sensor technology. Guan Ming also presented a poster on the development of the muon trigger detector for the muEDM experiment at PSI. More details about the workshop can be found at https://conference-indico.kek.jp/event/317/.
