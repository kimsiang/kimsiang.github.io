Kim Siang represented the accelerator-based particle physics experimental group in the annual report to the Key Laboratory of Shanghai and the MOE. In his presentation, he described the group’s progress in its three main directions: collider experiments (ATLAS and CEPC), the fixed-target experiment (Dark SHINE), and storage ring experiments (Muon g-2 and muEDM). Much progress was made in 2023, and we hope for more in 2024!
