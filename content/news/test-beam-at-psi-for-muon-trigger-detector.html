From late October to early November 2024, our group took part in a test beam campaign at PSI to evaluate the performance of the prototype muon trigger detector developed for the muEDM experiment. This was our second beam test at PSI for the muEDM project, in which we tested an improved design tailored for operation in high magnetic fields.

A huge shoutout to Tianqi Hu and Guan Ming Wong for their hard work and dedication in building this cutting-edge, highly reliable muon trigger detector. Their efforts have been instrumental in advancing the detector’s performance under challenging experimental conditions. We will analyze the collected data over the next few months to evaluate the detector’s performance.
