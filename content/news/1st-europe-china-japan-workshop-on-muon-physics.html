Our group participated in the 1st Europe-China-Japan Workshop on Muon Physics, held at the Minhang campus of Shanghai Jiao Tong University on 24–25 March 2025. Kim Siang gave a talk on the SHINE Muon Source, presenting the latest developments of the project. Afterwards, together with collaborators at SHINE, participants toured SSRF and SHINE. The workshop was very successful and sparked many interesting discussions on muon physics. More information about the workshop can be found here: https://indico-tdli.sjtu.edu.cn/event/3895/.
