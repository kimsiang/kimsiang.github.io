At Xiamen University Malaysia (XMUM), team members gave talks and detector demonstrations on cosmic-ray physics, muon detection, data acquisition and image analysis, and met with university representatives and China Communications Construction Company (Malaysia).

The three parties reached an initial agreement to work together on bringing muon imaging to local infrastructure projects in Malaysia — from tunnels and roads to other structures where non-destructive inspection matters.

This visit was part of the group’s Global Challenge Program 2026 trip across Peninsular Malaysia (4–11 August). Read the full trip summary, or the report in Chinese on SJTU News.
