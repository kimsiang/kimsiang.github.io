Our group recently participated in the first Workshop on Muon Science Technology and Industry (MELODY 2023), held from 3 to 6 November 2023 at the China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS). Kim Siang presented our group’s work on the Shanghai Muon Source, the Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment, and the PSI muEDM experiment. None of this progress would have been possible without the hard work of all our group members! More information about the workshop can be found here: https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/20601/.
