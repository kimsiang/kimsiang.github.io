Our group recently participated in the first Workshop on Muon Science Technology and Industry (MELODY2023) at the China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS). Kim Siang presented our group’s efforts in the Shanghai Muon Source, the Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment and the PSI muEDM experiment. All the progress would not be possible without the hardwork of all the group members! More information about the workshop can be found here: https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/20601/.
