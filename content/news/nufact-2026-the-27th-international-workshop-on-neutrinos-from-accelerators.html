From 31 August to 5 September 2026, our group hosted NuFact 2026, the 27th International Workshop on Neutrinos from Accelerators, at the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute in Shanghai. The workshop was chaired by Kim Siang Khaw on behalf of the Local Organizing Committee.

NuFact is the annual flagship meeting of the neutrino-factory and muon-collider community, held since 1999. It reviews progress on current and future facilities for studying neutral- and charged-lepton flavour violation and for searching for new phenomena. The programme was organised into seven working groups: neutrino oscillations, neutrino scattering, accelerator physics, muon physics, neutrinos beyond PMNS, detectors, and inclusion, diversity, equity, education and outreach.

We thank all speakers, participants, the international and local committees, and the TDLI staff and student volunteers who made the week possible.
