Our group recently participated in the Workshop on Scientific Applications and Key Technologies of the SHINE Electron Beam (SHINE电子束流科学应用与关键技术研讨会) on April 1–2. Kim Siang gave a talk on the SHINE-based muon source and its applications. Several other TDLI members also attended, and we enjoyed a comprehensive tour of the SHINE facility. We are all very excited about the progress of SHINE’s construction!
