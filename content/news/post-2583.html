Jiangtao Wang recently participated in the Particle Physics and Nuclear Physics Shanghai Symposium 2025, held at the University of Shanghai for Science and Technology (上海理工大学) from December 26 to 28, 2025. Representing the Muon Physics Group at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), Jiangtao gave a presentation titled “SHINE Muon Source and its Applications”, highlighting the group’s work on muon beams and muon physics.

Our group has been developing a high-repetition-rate muon source using the SHINE facility’s electron beam. Early results indicate a yield of about 10 million muons per second, which would support applications in μSR studies and muon tomography. We are preparing a feasibility test in 2026 to verify the muon yield and polarization, together with a demonstration of muon tomography using a high-energy muon beam from muon pair production.

The symposium offered an excellent platform for collaboration and knowledge sharing, and a valuable chance to present our research progress and connect with fellow physicists in the region.
