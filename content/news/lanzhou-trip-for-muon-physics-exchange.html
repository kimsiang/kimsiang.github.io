On March 3–4, Kim Siang visited Lanzhou for the first time for an academic exchange on muography with Prof. Zhiyi Liu’s group, accompanied by several officials from the Shanghai government. He also gave a seminar on muon physics at the Institute of Modern Physics (IMP), CAS. We enjoyed a comprehensive and truly eye-opening tour of IMP’s accelerator facility. The trip was a great success: we learned a great deal about muography from Prof. Liu’s group and had extensive discussions about future collaborations with both Lanzhou University and IMP. We look forward to our next visit!
