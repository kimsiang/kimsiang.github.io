Our group actively participated in the prestigious Simon Eidelman School on Muon Dipole Moments and Hadronic Effects, hosted by the Kobayashi-Maskawa Institute at Nagoya University from September 2 to 6, 2024. Yusuke Takeuchi, Siew Yan Hoh, Yonghao Zeng, and Fangchao Liu presented their recent research on the SHINE Muon Source and the Muon g-2 experiment in the poster session. Notably, Siew Yan Hoh’s work on the energy calibration of the Muon g-2 calorimeter won the Best Poster Award!

More information about the school can be found at https://indico.kmi.nagoya-u.ac.jp/event/8/overview.
