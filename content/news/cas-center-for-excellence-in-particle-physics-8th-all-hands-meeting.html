Kim-Siang presented the current status of the Fermilab Muon g-2 experiment at the 8th All-Hands Meeting of the CAS Center for Excellence in Particle Physics (中国科学院粒子物理前沿卓越创新中心, 第八次全体会议), held at IHEP on December 4, 2020. Due to COVID travel restrictions, the talk was given via Zoom. Details about the meeting can be found here: https://indico.ihep.ac.cn/event/12962/.
