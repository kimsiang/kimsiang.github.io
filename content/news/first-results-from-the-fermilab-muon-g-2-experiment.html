After almost three years of analyzing the Run 1 data taken in spring 2018, our collaboration has published the first results on the muon anomalous magnetic moment (muon g-2) in a series of Physical Review papers.

  • Muon g-2 (Phys. Rev. Lett.): https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.126.141801
  • Magnetic Field Measurement (Phys. Rev. A): https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.103.042208
  • Muon Anomalous Precession Frequency Measurement (Phys. Rev. D): https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevD.103.072002
  • Beam Dynamics Corrections for Run 1: https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.03240 (published in Phys. Rev. Accel. Beams: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevAccelBeams.24.044002)
