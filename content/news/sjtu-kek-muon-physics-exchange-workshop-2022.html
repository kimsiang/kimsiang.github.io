Our group co-organized the SJTU-KEK Muon Physics Exchange Workshop 2022, together with Prof. Liang Li from SPA and Prof. Tsutomu Mibe from KEK/Univ. of Tokyo. The workshop was very successful and we enjoyed a lot sharing progress from both sides on various muon physics experiments. More information about the workshop can be found here: https://indico-tdli.sjtu.edu.cn/event/1170/.
