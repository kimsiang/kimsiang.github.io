Our group co-organized the SJTU-KEK Muon Physics Exchange Workshop 2022, held online on 29 August 2022, together with Prof. Liang Li (SPA) and Prof. Tsutomu Mibe (KEK/University of Tokyo). The workshop was very successful, and we greatly enjoyed sharing progress from both sides on various muon physics experiments. More information about the workshop can be found here: https://indico-tdli.sjtu.edu.cn/event/1170/.
