Kim Siang was recently invited to give an overview talk on electromagnetic dipole moments at the ICFA Seminar 2023 on “Future Perspectives in High-Energy Physics”, held at DESY in Hamburg from November 27 to December 1, 2023. The seminar is an international exchange of information focusing on plans for future facilities in particle physics. Attendance is by invitation only, and participants include the directors of most of the world’s major laboratories in the field, senior particle and accelerator physicists, and government science officials from several countries. Please visit the link for more details about the meeting: https://indico.desy.de/event/38293/.
