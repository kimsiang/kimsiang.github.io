Yonghao recently participated in the Chinese Muography Workshop 2024, hosted by the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in Hefei from November 7 to 9, 2024. Representing our group, he presented the latest progress of the Shanghai Muography Project. The workshop brought together researchers from institutes and universities across China to exchange insights on recent developments in muography technologies and their wide-ranging applications, providing a platform for experts in muon detection, electronics, and imaging algorithms to foster collaborations, explore new ideas, and set research directions. One of the highlights was the USTC team’s presentation of their “Tank+Missile” muon detector system, which drew significant attention for its design and its graphical user interface providing real-time visualization of muon tracks, demonstrating the potential of user-friendly, interactive applications in muography. A major milestone of the workshop was the establishment of the Chinese Muography Collaboration Group, marking a new stage of coordinated effort among researchers in the field that is expected to drive muography research forward and expand its applications across disciplines. For more details about the workshop, visit https://indico.pnp.ustc.edu.cn/event/2930/overview; recordings of the talks are available at https://www.koushare.com/live/details/38448.
