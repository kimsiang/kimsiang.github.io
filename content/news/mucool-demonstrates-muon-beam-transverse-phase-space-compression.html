The muCool collaboration, of which Kim-Siang has been a member since 2011, has published results demonstrating the transverse part of muon-beam phase-space compression. The paper was highlighted as an Editors’ Suggestion in Phys. Rev. Lett. and can be found at https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.125.164802 (arXiv: 2003.11986).
